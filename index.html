Mechanical signals. Visible uncertainty.

A research tool built around saved GLD and gold-market experiments.

01

Freeze the rules

The board evaluates the documented signal criteria. Alternative entry times, strike distances and exits do not become additional confirmations.

02

Keep overlap honest

Related checks share a driver. Directional signals and weak experimental checks are reported separately. Driver groups are a research taxonomy, not proof of statistical independence.

03

Check the clock and the feed

A rule stays waiting before its observation time. Missing, stale, future-dated or invalid inputs cannot produce a green light. An ended entry window expires the signal.

04

Preserve the contrary evidence

Each card pairs the trigger with the saved results and limitations. Midpoint marks, incomplete exit coverage, small samples and failed follow-ups remain visible.

What this version does

Evaluates a supplied morning snapshot, explains each criterion, groups overlapping signals, and saves an auditable observation log. The demonstration uses invented inputs. The import contract supports a read-only market-data export without exposing brokerage credentials.

What it does not infer: live quote availability, guaranteed fills, profitable option pricing, or a combined strategy edge. No orders are submitted. Option returns in the research summaries are returns on premium spent, not account returns.
